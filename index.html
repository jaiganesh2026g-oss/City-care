About CityCare Platform

CityCare bridges the gap between urban residents and local municipal infrastructure teams.

Complaints submitted via CityCare are instantly digitized and routed to the corresponding zonal municipal department. High-priority issues are typically reviewed within 4 hours.
No registration is required! You can file complaints immediately by entering your name and location. You receive a unique Complaint ID (e.g. CC-2026-0001) to track progress anytime.
When field inspectors complete work on-site, they mark the complaint as "Resolved" in the Admin Dashboard, which immediately updates your tracking page status timeline.
